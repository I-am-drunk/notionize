# Inference protocol findings

This note summarizes the privacy-safe output generated by `reference/scripts/extract_inference_protocol.py`. Prompts, IDs, personal context values, response prose, URLs, request headers, cookies, raw bodies, and record-map contents are omitted.

It remains separate hook-only evidence and is intentionally not inserted into `inference-protocol.json`; hook and HAR totals therefore must not be added as if every record were distinct.

## Transport

All seven captured HAR calls use:

- `POST /api/v3/runInferenceTranscript`
- Request MIME: `application/json`
- Response status: `200`
- Response MIME: `application/x-ndjson`
- Patch response mode enabled with `patchResponseVersion: 2`

The response is newline-delimited JSON. The five successful observations start with `patch-start`, emit one or more `patch` records, emit one `record-map`, and end with one `patch-sync` containing the final state array. Every successful stream replays exactly to its terminal sync.

The two live quota-rejected observations are also HTTP 200 NDJSON, but they contain only `patch-start` and `record-map`. Their initial state contains the `premium-feature-unavailable` discriminator and feature-availability metadata. They contain no patch operations, terminal `patch-sync`, final `agent-inference`, or terminal token usage. The extractor consequently classifies them as `application_error`, never as successful responses. A missing `patch-sync` without that allowlisted discriminator would instead remain `incomplete_or_unclassified`; missing terminal state alone is not proof of quota failure.

Some HAR exporters marked response content as base64 even when it was already plain NDJSON. The extractor handles both representations by recognizing plain JSON first and otherwise performing strict base64 decoding.

## Request transcript shape

The requests send an ordered `transcript` array containing typed steps:

- `config`: workflow type, model selection, web-search setting, search-scope types, and available connector names.
- `context`: a structured context object. Only its field names are retained in the safe output.
- `updated-config`: present for the explicit-medium continuation override.
- `user`: user content. Only its character count is retained.

All captured inference requests use `saveAllThreadOperations: true`, `setUnreadState: true`, and `threadType: workflow`. The earlier probes use `createdSource: workflows`; the live long-history and quota attempts use `createdSource: ai_module`. They enable web search over the `everything` scope and advertise the `notion-calendar` connector.

The auto and explicit no-thinking probes create new threads and titles (`createThread: true`, `generateTitle: true`) and send complete transcripts (`isPartialTranscript: false`). The explicit-medium probe and all three live-HAR entries continue an existing thread (`createThread: false`, `generateTitle: false`) and send partial transcripts (`isPartialTranscript: true`). The no-thinking selection is carried directly in `config`; the medium selection is carried by `updated-config`. The successful live request contains 34,045 user-message characters; the extractor retains that count, not the text.

## Patch behavior and context loading

The auto stream contains 15 NDJSON records: 1 `patch-start`, 12 `patch`, 1 `record-map`, and 1 `patch-sync`. Its 23 patch operations use operation codes `a` (20) and `x` (3). The stream appends an `agent-turn-full-record-map`, three `agent-tool-result` steps, a `config`, a `title`, and an `agent-inference` step before synchronizing final state. Replaying all 23 operations from `patch-start.data` exactly reproduces `patch-sync.data`.

The no-thinking stream also contains 15 NDJSON records: 1 `patch-start`, 12 `patch`, 1 `record-map`, and 1 `patch-sync`. Its 23 operations use `a` (19) and `x` (4). It appends an `agent-turn-full-record-map`, three `agent-tool-result` steps, an `agent-inference`, and a `title`. Replaying all operations exactly reproduces its final sync.

The three auto-load results in each new/full-thread trace are applied `callFunction` operations:

| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| `connections.fs.readFiles` | `core_docs` | 16 ms | 11 ms |
| `connections.fs.readFiles` | `user_docs` | 15 ms | 11 ms |
| `connections.notion.loadUser` | `context_urls` | 28 ms | 23 ms |

The explicit-medium stream contains 9 NDJSON records: 1 `patch-start`, 6 `patch`, 1 `record-map`, and 1 `patch-sync`. Its 17 patch operations use `a` (14) and `x` (3), append one `agent-inference` step, and contain no tool-result steps. Replaying all 17 operations exactly reproduces the final sync in the explicit capture, its settled duplicate, and their sanitized counterparts.

The live long-history success contains 8 NDJSON records: 1 `patch-start`, 5 `patch`, 1 `record-map`, and 1 `patch-sync`. Its 16 operations use `a` (14) and `x` (2), append one `agent-inference` step, and replay exactly to the terminal sync. The two subsequent quota attempts contain two records apiece (`patch-start`, `record-map`) and no patch operations or terminal state, so patch reconstruction is correctly marked not attempted.

Across the four distinct successful traces, the `x` operations target the inference content path: three extensions for auto, three for medium, four for no-thinking, and two for the live long-history success. Operation `a` adds/appends a supplied value and clears a target when the operation has no `v` member; an explicit JSON null remains a value. For a completed stream, the final `patch-sync` state is the authoritative assembled result. Record-map payloads contain thread records; the safe output retains only table counts.

## Executable compatibility proof

`reference/tools/notion-anthropic-sse/` implements the matrix's strict buffered text-only subset. Independent local validation produced:

- 24 of 24 unit tests passing;
- discovery of seven endpoint entries across all six sanitized HARs;
- successful conversion of five HAR observations, representing four distinct successful transactions after deduplicating the settled medium capture;
- rejection of both quota bodies before any Anthropic SSE frame is emitted;
- 9 SSE frames for auto, 9 for medium, 10 for no-thinking, 9 for the settled duplicate, and 8 for the live long-history success; and
- exact agreement between incremental text, applied patch state, terminal `patch-sync`, and projected Notion-reported usage.

The adapter deliberately buffers through `patch-sync`, retimes terminal input/cache values into `message_start`, re-emits cumulative input/cache/output usage at `message_delta` for SDK 0.121.0 accumulator correction, and labels its `end_turn` stop reason as synthetic. It also emits explicit nulls for SDK contract fields that Notion does not supply. An application-error stream is rejected instead of being fabricated into a successful Anthropic response. The adapter does not convert request history, partial-continuation semantics, thinking, native tools, compaction, application errors, or cancellation, and it does not claim provider authenticity or a lossless round trip.

## Important limits

- The auto and medium HAR `time` fields (388 ms and 124 ms) are shorter than their server-reported time-to-first-token metrics, while the no-thinking HAR time covers a larger interval. HAR export timing and server timing should not be treated as the same measurement.
- Five HARs contain seven inference observations. The settled capture duplicates medium, leaving six distinct HAR transactions: four successes and two quota errors.
- The hook-only short success is separate from the HAR dataset. Across sources there are five distinct successful transactions and two quota failures, but `inference-protocol.json` intentionally contains only the seven HAR-backed observations (four distinct successes, one duplicated-success observation, and two distinct failures).
- These traces establish one endpoint and one patch-stream protocol, not every Notion AI mode, tool, model, error path, retry, cancellation path, or attachment flow.
- No endpoint behavior was replayed; findings come from the already captured local HAR bodies only.
